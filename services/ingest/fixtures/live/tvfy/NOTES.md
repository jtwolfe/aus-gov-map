# They Vote For You fixtures

Short **published** excerpt of Commonwealth divisions from
[They Vote For You](https://theyvoteforyou.org.au/help/api).

Used when the live API is blocked or `--path` points here. Named votes
are a handful of published member positions, not a full chamber list.
Counts (`aye_votes` / `no_votes`) are as published on TVFY for that
division. Do not treat this file as a complete division.

Attribute They Vote For You (OpenAustralia Foundation) and the
Commonwealth Hansard record they cite.

Do not invent vote positions.
